## Lineage and dependencies

PSA census collection and processing → regional XLSX publication on the PSA Population and Housing page → checksum-verified immutable raw storage → visible-sheet extraction with file, sheet, and row lineage → hierarchy reconstruction and parent-total reconciliation → normalized barangay population table → PSGC-scoped geographic integration → downstream accessibility denominators and population coverage indicators.

## Known quality problems

Summary only; evidence lives in [profile.md](profile.md).

- **Observed:** no PSGC or other geographic codes are supplied; names alone are not globally unique.
- **Observed:** 4,004 barangay names repeat, covering 20,136 rows. Names are unique only when scoped to region, sheet, and parent area.
- **Observed:** 2,775 barangay source names have trailing whitespace.
- **Observed:** hierarchy levels share one column and depend on workbook layout. The parser is validated by zero parent-total and sheet-total reconciliation failures.
- **Observed:** 6 explicitly hidden calculation sheets are present. Some contain checks and `#REF!` formula results; they are not published geographic tables and are excluded by workbook visibility state.
- **Observed:** 12 barangays have a population of zero. These are retained and must not be converted to missing.
- **Observed:** the 42,011 barangays differ by one from the 42,010 barangays in PSGC 2Q 2026, confirming that geographic-version alignment is required before joining.
- **Observed:** the file names contain download suffixes such as `_0`, `_1`, and `_2`; those suffixes are not region identifiers.
- **Suspected:** superscript footnote markers are embedded in some geographic labels and may appear as ordinary trailing digits in text-only extraction.

## Limitations for analysis

- This is a single 2024 census snapshot. It does not support annual population trends or intercensal growth without other releases.
- Counts are total population only. No age, sex, household, urban/rural, or other demographic breakdown is included.
- The 1,708 overseas census population is present in the national total but cannot be assigned to regions or barangays.
- Zero-population barangays require contextual review before use as denominators.
- No coordinates, boundaries, PSGC codes, or explicit geographic-level column are supplied.
- Name-only integration is vulnerable to punctuation, whitespace, footnotes, renaming, splits, transfers, and repeated place names.
- The source places Sulu under BARMM for 01 July 2024, while PSGC 2Q 2026 places Sulu under Region IX. Region must not be overwritten silently during crosswalking.

## Recommended controls and monitoring

- Fail ingestion if any expected file is absent, a checksum changes, a visible sheet lacks the published header, a barangay population is blank or non-integer, or a candidate key duplicates.
- Require every parent and sheet total to equal the sum of extracted children; the current delivery has zero failures.
- Preserve raw geographic labels alongside trimmed matching fields. Never overwrite source text.
- Exclude sheets only when their workbook state is explicitly hidden, and log every excluded sheet by file and name.
- Compare regional file coverage, visible and hidden sheet counts, barangay counts, zero counts, and population totals on every delivery.
- Join to PSGC within parent geography, record match method and PSGC version, and quarantine unmatched or ambiguous rows.
- Reconcile the sum of geographic totals to the official national total and document the overseas-population difference.
- Obtain publisher confirmation of superscript footnote semantics, file-version naming, release cadence, and reuse terms.
